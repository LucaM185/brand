The decision often exists. It is split across a specification, a workbook, a schematic, and a thread. We connect those without moving the files out of the company.

Questions the research desk is built to answer

  • Which revision The schematic on the bench is not always the one the spreadsheet lists as released.
  • Where a tag lives A device tag in Eplan, the same name in an export, and a different spelling in the mail that approved the change.
  • What was agreed A commitment made in email and never copied into the Word specification.
  • What the sheet is calculating The column everyone trusts, traced back to the cells and the message that defined it.

Left in place

Word files, workbooks, Eplan projects, and mail stay in the systems that already hold them. Research points at those sources. It does not create a second library that goes stale.

An answer with a citation

A useful result names the file, the sheet, the page, and the message. Someone can open the original and disagree. That is the point.
