Workflow optimization for manufacturing companies.

We follow one job through planning, the line, and quality, and remove the wait that is not on any official chart.

Where time goes

Status is retyped from a sheet into another sheet. A hold is cleared by phone and never written down. The work instruction on the bench is not the revision planning released.

What changes

One handoff gets a single record and a named owner. The people who run the job help write the new path, and we stay while the first weeks show what we missed.

Typical paths

  • Order release From the plan to the first operation, including the check that usually happens in a mailbox.
  • Changeover What must be true before the line restarts, and who confirms it.
  • Quality hold How a defect is raised, where it waits, and how the release is recorded.
  • Maintenance request From the symptom on the floor to the work that was actually done.
  • Shift handover The few facts the next crew cannot rediscover from memory.
