Workflow optimization for Rockwell PLC projects.

A controller project is a workflow: who may change it, which revision is in the cabinet, and which document is supposed to describe it.

We work on the path around Studio 5000 projects and the plant documents that travel with them. Safety-rated logic stays under your existing safety process. We do not bypass site access rules, and we do not download to a controller outside the change path you already require.

  • Project hygiene Tasks, programs, tag names, and a revision note someone else can read six months later.
  • The change path How a request becomes a reviewed edit, a download, and a record of what is now running.
  • The words beside the logic Alarm text, HMI labels, and the checklist use the same names as the tags.
  • FAT and SAT The test follows the project revision, not a checklist copied from the last machine.
  • The surrounding files The Eplan page, the I/O sheet, and the mail that approved the change stay attached to that revision.
