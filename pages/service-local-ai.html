Local AI inside your company.

Search, classification, and drafts on hardware you control. Project files are not sent to a public model service. A person still signs, sends, and downloads.

Jobs worth giving a model

Find the procedure that matches a symptom. Classify incoming mail and sheet rows. Draft a work instruction from the documents you already trust. Each answer points at the file it used.

Jobs we leave with people

Releasing a drawing, approving a download to a controller, and telling a customer the work is done. The model can prepare the material. It does not own the action.

What “local” means here

  • On your network The model runs on a server the company administers, or on a machine in the plant.
  • Sources stay put Word, Excel, Eplan exports, and mail are read where they already live.
  • A citation, not a paragraph alone If the source cannot be shown, the answer is not ready to use.
  • A named owner Someone is responsible for what the system is allowed to see and for what people do with the output.
