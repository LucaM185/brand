Home / Services / DOCX translation

DOCX translation that keeps the document’s structure.

The delivered file is the same Word document, in the other language. Headings, tables, numbering, and styles stay. The wording follows the company glossary.

What stays

Styles, the heading outline, numbered lists, tables and merged cells, headers and footers, captions, and text in text boxes. Cross-references stay linked. Pictures stay where they were. We do not paste the translation into a blank file.

What the glossary decides

The preferred term in the target language. The name that must not be translated: a product, a tag, a site, a controller. The abbreviation people already use, and the synonym that is no longer allowed.

Where the text is replaced

  • Body and headings Paragraphs and heading text, in the style they already have.
  • Tables Each cell, including repeating header rows. Column widths and merged cells are left alone.
  • Headers, footers, captions Including the caption on a figure and the text in a content control, when that text is meant to be read.
  • Left untranslated on purpose Drawing numbers, tag names, file names, and any term the glossary marks as fixed.

How we work

01

Freeze the glossary

Preferred terms, terms that stay as written, and the words people keep arguing about. The glossary is yours. A general dictionary does not override it.

02

Translate inside the file

Text is replaced in the existing .docx. Styles, numbering, and layout are not rebuilt.

03

Record what the glossary missed

A term that was not listed is flagged, not guessed a second way in the next file. You add it before the following document.

Send the glossary and one representative file.

A procedure, a specification, or a test record is enough to see whether the structure will hold.
